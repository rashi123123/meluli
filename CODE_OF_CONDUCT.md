# Code of Conduct

Meluli is built for people who write in Hebrew, and its community is open to anyone who wants to help, in Hebrew or in English. This code applies to issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews and any other space the project runs.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source, to macOS development or to Hebrew.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Say what you would change and why.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding that someone meant harm.
- Respect people's privacy. Never post someone else's recordings, transcripts, messages or personal details, and remove your own before you share a log or screenshot.
- Credit other people's work, and say when code or text comes from somewhere else.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Language or images that demean anyone for their background, identity, religion, nationality, language, age, disability or body.
- Unwanted sexual attention or content.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, and say that it is a conduct report. Only the maintainer can read it, and it stays confidential. GitHub's **Report content** option on a comment or issue also works, and goes to GitHub's own staff. You will not be penalized for a report made in good faith.

## What happens next

The maintainer may remove content, lock a thread, or warn, suspend or ban a participant, according to how serious and how repeated the behaviour is. Decisions are explained to the people involved where that is safe.

This code is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
